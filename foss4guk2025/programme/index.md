The full programme for FOSS4G:UK 2025 will be added here once the [call for talks](https://uk.osgeo.org/foss4guk2025/callfortalks/) has closed on Friday 18th July. In the meantime, here's an outline (exact timings may change):

* 10:00: Conference opens
* 17:00: Conference closes
* 20:00: Social event, central Leeds

### Thursday, 2nd October
* 09:30: Conference opens
* 16:30: Conference closes
