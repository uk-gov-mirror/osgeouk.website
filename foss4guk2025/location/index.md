The conference location will be [Horizon Leeds](https://horizonleeds.co.uk/) - a modern and fully-accessible venue in the centre of town, part of the NHS Conferderation. As well as a theatre-style main space, there are two other large meeting rooms suitable for workshops or smaller groups, and a central catering and seating area for networking, sponsors and lunch/coffee. 
It's under ten minutes walk from Leeds railway station, and there's a [25% discount available](https://www.lner.co.uk/tickets-savings/savings-rewards/special-offers/deals/conference-leeds/) on train travel to the event - we'll be including detals of this in the registration page. 
There are plenty of bars, coffee shops and restaurants in the area, and there will be an optional social event on the first evening at a venue close to the conference - details to follow.
