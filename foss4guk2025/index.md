OSGeo:UK is happy to announce that following a series of successful distributed, online and local conferences over the past few years, we are ready for a full two-day event again in - and it will be in Leeds on the 1st and 2nd of October 2025. 

We have booked the state of the art [Horizon](https://horizonleeds.co.uk/) venue in the heart of the city, and will shortly be publishing more details on registration, call for papers and other details.
A selection of sponsorship opportunities are available, and we'll be publicising our sponsors as soon as they sign up, so get touch now to maximise your exposure.
In the meantime, save the date, and follow us on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/osgeo-uk/), [Mastodon](https://fosstodon.org/@osgeouk), [Twitter/X](https://twitter.com/osgeouk), [join the mailing list](https://lists.osgeo.org/mailman/listinfo/uk), [subscribe to the newsletter](https://stats.sender.net/forms/b4160d/view), and [contact us by email](mailto:osgeouk@gmail.com)
